### Introduction
Boardman, OR is a major data center
hub, with most being owned by
Amazon Web Services. The current
locations of these centers are
represented with blue points in the
images to the left. Data centers are
known to generate large amounts of
waste, especially in terms of heat.
These images depict the city as well
as urban heat islands at two different
dates before and after major data
center developments. It can be
observed that the urban heat island
(UHI) index in proximate areas has
increased following the development
of data centers. 
<br><br>
### Methods and Results
The study area of the project is the city of Boardman, Oregon.
Current Amazon data
center addresses in the city were geocoded for
visual representation. Landsat-8
Collection 2 datasets of bands 10, 4,
3, and 2 from 7/5/13 and 7/3/24
capturing Boardman were retrieved
via USGS Earth Explorer. All datasets
have a 30m resolution. Bands 4, 3,
and 2 were combined to create a set
of before and after composite natural
images. Band 10 was scaled and
used to create an UHI index for before
and after comparison. The index was
calculated as: (Land Surface Temp –
mean Land Surface Temp) / standard
deviation of Land Surface Temp.
